How we work

Four things you can count on

  • The price on the list

    What you see on the price list is what you pay. Colour work starts at the listed price; we tell you the final amount before we begin.

  • Clean tools

    Metal tools are cleaned and sterilised after every client. Files and buffers are used once.

  • Patch test first

    First time with lash or brow tint? We test a small spot 48 hours before the appointment.

  • Free changes up to 24 hours

    Move or cancel your appointment at no cost if you tell us at least 24 hours ahead.
