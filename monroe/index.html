How to get a time

  1. Pick a treatment

    Everything is on the price list with its duration. Booking several? Add up the times and tell us the total.

  2. Call or message us

    By phone or WhatsApp, Tuesday to Saturday. Name the treatment and a few times that suit you.

  3. Get a confirmation

    We confirm by message. Plans changed? Let us know 24 hours ahead and we move or cancel the appointment at no cost.
